# Soccer model board, 2026-11-08

Our own model (soccer_model.py): time-weighted team ratings for goals, shots on target and corners from football-data.co.uk, expected goals = 60% goals model + 40% shots-on-target model, Dixon-Coles score grid. **Backtest (2023-26, 5 leagues, 5,256 games, walk-forward): the closing market beats it** (1X2 log loss 0.986 vs 0.964; over/under 2.5 0.676 vs 0.666), and betting its 3%+ edges at average closing odds lost 13% (model) and 17% (50/50 blend with the market). Corners projections barely beat the league average. So this board is a research aid and a fair-price reference for DraftKings-only markets, not a source of picks. 'Gap' = model minus market, points.
